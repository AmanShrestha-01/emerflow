# Questions to ask NotebookLM after adding notebooklm-source.md

Paste `notebooklm-source.md` into NotebookLM as a source, then try these.

## To understand the project
1. Explain Concord to me like I have never worked in healthcare or software.
2. Walk me through what happens to one patient, step by step, from the moment
   the red button is pressed.
3. What is the difference between "find every contradiction" and "find the
   contradictions that matter right now"? Why does that difference matter?
4. Why was artificial intelligence necessary here, and which parts of the system
   deliberately do not use AI?

## To prepare for judges
5. Act as a skeptical engineering judge. Ask me the five hardest questions about
   this project, then tell me what a good answer to each would sound like.
6. What is the weakest part of this project, and how should it be presented
   honestly?
7. Write a ninety-second spoken script for demonstrating this at a science fair.

## To explain it to a teammate
8. Summarize what gets built and what deliberately does not get built, and
   explain the reasoning behind each decision not to build something.
9. Make a checklist of the seven components, in the order they should be built.

## For an audio overview
10. Generate an audio overview aimed at someone with no technical background who
    wants to understand why records disagreeing is dangerous.
