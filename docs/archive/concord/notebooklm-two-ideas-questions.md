# Questions to ask NotebookLM after adding notebooklm-two-ideas.md

## To understand each idea
1. Explain Hospital Swarm Commander to me as though I know nothing about
   hospitals or software.
2. Explain DeepChart the same way.
3. Why is it more dangerous for a doctor to have too much conflicting
   information than too little information?
4. Walk me through the tangle in the intensive care bed example, step by step.

## To understand the technical parts
5. What is discrete-event simulation, and why does it conflict with showing a
   live demonstration?
6. Explain the seven kinds of disagreement between medical records, with an
   example of each.
7. Explain "absence of evidence is not evidence of absence" in this context, and
   why it is the hardest problem in the field.
8. Why must a language model not be used for the bed-assignment arithmetic?

## To compare them
9. Make a table comparing the two projects on: what they ask, whether the AI is
   essential, what can be verified, and what can be faked.
10. In which project is the AI doing real work, and in which is it decorative?
    Explain why.
11. Explain why combining the two ideas fixes a weakness in each.

## To prepare for hard questions
12. Act as a skeptical engineering judge. Ask the five hardest questions about
    each project, then give a good answer to each.
13. What is the single biggest weakness of each idea, stated honestly?

## For an audio overview
14. Generate an audio overview comparing the two projects for someone with no
    technical background.
